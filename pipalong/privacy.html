Pipalong Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026.

Short version: Pipalong has no account, no tracking, no analytics and no server. Your reminders, history and calendar stay on your Mac. We collect no personal data.

What stays on your Mac

Your settings, reminder schedule, water log and history are saved only on your Mac, inside Pipalong's private app storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else. If you export your history as a CSV file, it is saved only where you choose.

Calendar

If you allow calendar access, Pipalong reads the times, titles, locations and attendee counts of your upcoming events on your Mac to remind you before meetings. It also looks in an event's notes and location for a video-call link (for example Teams, Zoom or Google Meet) so it can show a Join button; the notes themselves are not saved. Calendar data never leaves your Mac.

If you paste a calendar link (an https:// or webcal:// address), Pipalong downloads that calendar directly from the address you provided, over an encrypted connection. The link often contains a private token, so it is stored only on your Mac.

Staying quiet during calls and while you type

To avoid interrupting you, Pipalong checks whether your microphone or camera is currently in use, which app is in front, whether a full-screen window is showing, and how long ago a key was pressed. It never records, reads or stores audio, video, screen contents or what you type.

Notifications

If you turn on Notification Center banners, macOS asks for your permission. Banners are created on your Mac.

Purchases

Pipalong Plus is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. Pipalong only checks with Apple whether Plus is active on your Apple ID.

Children

Pipalong does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting Pipalong and its data from your Mac removes everything it stored. There is nothing stored with us to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
