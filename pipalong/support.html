Pipalong Support

Need help? Email pipalong.app@gmail.com and include your macOS version and Pipalong version (Settings ▸ About). We usually reply within two working days.

Where is the app?

Pipalong lives in the menu bar at the top of your screen. Click the small character to open it. It has no Dock icon by default.

I'm not getting meeting reminders

Open Pipalong ▸ Settings ▸ Calendar and allow calendar access, or paste a calendar link. Work Outlook calendars appear once your account is added in macOS System Settings ▸ Internet Accounts, or when you paste a published Outlook calendar link. A link published as "busy times only" has no meeting titles or Join links.

Reminders aren't walking in

Free includes one walk-on a day; the rest appear as bubbles. Walk-ons also pause during calls, in full-screen apps and when Reduce Motion is on. Check Settings ▸ Character ▸ Walk-ons, including "Quiet bubbles only".

Restore Pipalong Plus

Open Settings ▸ About ▸ Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with. To cancel a yearly plan, use Settings ▸ About ▸ Manage subscription, or the App Store's subscription settings.
